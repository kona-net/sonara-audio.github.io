# SONARA Image Policy — V2

All product-card and product-detail images in V2 are local generated assets.

Rules:
- No screenshots of other websites.
- No price labels inside photos.
- No product names inside photos.
- No stars, badges, buttons, hearts, or UI inside photos.
- One product per image.
- Product is centered and fully visible.
- Interface text stays outside the photograph.
- Product image assignments are deterministic through `data/catalog.json`.
