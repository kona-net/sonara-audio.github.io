# SONARA Audio

> **Sound a Higher Life.**

SONARA Audio is a premium audio retail concept focused on high-quality
listening experiences for home, personal, professional, and
entertainment environments.

## Brand

**SONARA Audio** combines premium industrial design, immersive sound,
and a refined digital shopping experience.

-   **Palette:** Graphite Black, Deep Charcoal, Metallic Silver,
    Electric Blue
-   **Style:** Premium, technical, cinematic, precise
-   **Photography:** Realistic, sharp, professionally framed product and
    lifestyle photography
-   **Tagline:** **Sound a Higher Life.**

## Store Categories

-   🎧 **Headphones** --- Wireless, noise-cancelling, studio, over-ear
    and premium listening.
-   🔊 **Speakers** --- Portable, wireless, bookshelf, floor-standing
    and stereo systems.
-   🎚️ **Hi-Fi & Amplification** --- Amplifiers, DACs, receivers,
    preamps and high-fidelity components.
-   💿 **Turntables** --- Modern turntables, cartridges and vinyl
    accessories.
-   🎬 **Home Theater** --- Soundbars, subwoofers, surround systems and
    AV receivers.
-   🎤 **Studio Audio** --- Studio monitors, microphones, interfaces and
    creator equipment.
-   📡 **Streaming Audio** --- Network players, multi-room systems and
    high-resolution streaming.
-   🔌 **Accessories** --- Cables, stands, mounts, adapters, cases and
    audio essentials.

## Product Photography Standards

Product imagery is central to the SONARA identity.

Images should be:

-   Realistic
-   Sharp and high resolution
-   Professionally framed
-   Fully visible without accidental cropping
-   Free of prices, buttons, badges, ratings or website UI
-   Consistent within product cards
-   Optimized for web performance

Lifestyle photography should feature realistic living rooms, recording
studios, desktop setups, vinyl rooms, home theaters and minimalist
workspaces.

## Visual Design

SONARA uses a dark premium interface with electric-blue highlights.

-   **Graphite:** `#0B0E12`
-   **Charcoal:** `#151A21`
-   **Silver:** `#C8CDD3`
-   **Ice:** `#F4F7FA`
-   **Electric Blue:** `#2E8BFF`

The interface should use strong spacing, crisp borders, subtle
gradients, controlled shadows and restrained motion.
